--- title: Privacy description: What this site stores (almost nothing) and which third parties see requests. ---

Searches are sent from your browser to our lookup API, which forwards them to FMCSA's open-data service and caches results for ten minutes. We do not store what you search for, and there are no accounts.

The site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which see standard request logs (IP address, user agent). Google AdSense may serve ads and uses cookies as described in Google's partner-sites policy; you can opt out of personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. Partner links may set the partner's own cookies when clicked.

Carrier data shown on this site is FMCSA's public record. It is not personal data we collect; corrections to it go through FMCSA's DataQs system.
