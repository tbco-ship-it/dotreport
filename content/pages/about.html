--- title: About DOT Report Card description: Why this site exists and who it's for. ---

FMCSA publishes a remarkable amount of data about every motor carrier in the United States, but it publishes it as government tables spread across four sites, with no way to tell whether a 9% driver out-of-service rate is good or bad. This site turns that public record into one page per carrier with a grade, the national average next to every number, and the things a carrier can actually do about it.

It's built for owner-operators and small fleets who want to see their own record the way a broker or insurer sees it, and for anyone who needs a quick first read on a carrier before digging into SAFER.

The site is free. Some "what to do next" links are referral links to partners; they are marked, they don't affect grades, and they never reorder anything. See the methodology for how grades are computed.
