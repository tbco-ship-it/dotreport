This report card combines records from FMCSA's public Motor Carrier Census, SMS roadside inspection files, and Licensing & Insurance (L&I) extracts (benchmark file metadata updated {{ nat.sms_updated }}; not a verified observation cutoff). The letter grade is calculated using transparent mathematical deductions based on out-of-service deviations from the national average, fleet-proportional crash involvement and fatalities, and USDOT registration status; public flags, authority and insurance are informational only.

A safety report card is a preliminary screening tool and does not constitute a government safety rating, insurance warranty, or binding dispatch approval. For official government records, visit the SAFER Carrier Snapshot{% if static_page %} or the FMCSA SMS Overview{% endif %}.
