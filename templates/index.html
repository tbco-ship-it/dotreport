How the grade works

Full methodology

Every carrier starts at 100. Points come off for a driver or vehicle out-of-service rate above the national average ({{ nat.driver_oos_rate }}% / {{ nat.vehicle_oos_rate }}%, computed from the current SMS file), for each BASIC alert flag, for crashes relative to fleet size, for inactive operating authority and for a missing liability filing. Fewer than 5 inspections and we say so instead of pretending.
