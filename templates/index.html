Scoring methodology & benchmarks

Full grading methodology →

Our independent index is not an FMCSA rating or dispatch approval. Carriers need at least five driver inspections and five vehicle inspections, valid comparison benchmarks, crash/fleet data, and a known registration status to receive a grade. Otherwise the report shows NR — Not rated.

  • Out-of-service rates: Driver rate above {{ nat.driver_oos_rate }}%: −10; at least twice the benchmark: −25. Vehicle rate above {{ nat.vehicle_oos_rate }}%: −10; at least 1.5 times the benchmark: −25.
  • Crash involvement: −20 at 0.5 or more reported fatalities (people) per 100 power units; otherwise −15 above eight crash records per 100 units, or −5 above four. Records do not assign fault.
  • USDOT registration: −40 for inactive registration in the census extract. This is not a determination of operating authority.
  • Authority, insurance and SMS flags: Informational only. Missing data is not evidence of a lapse, and historical filings do not establish current coverage. No deductions apply to these fields.

Benchmarks are calculated as total out-of-service inspections divided by total inspections in the SMS file, multiplied by 100. File metadata updated {{ nat.sms_updated }}; this is not necessarily the observation cutoff. See all rules and limitations.
